# TenderAlert

TenderAlert is a Uganda-focused B2B tender intelligence platform.

It helps businesses discover relevant procurement opportunities, understand tender requirements, track deadlines, and receive personalized tender alerts.

## Development Status

TenderAlert V2 is currently under development.

## Core Product

- Tender discovery
- Tender matching
- AI-powered plain-language summaries
- Compliance guidance
- Deadline reminders
- WhatsApp alerts
- Mobile Money subscriptions
- Customer dashboard
- Owner/admin dashboard
- Procurement intelligence

## Target Market

Uganda initially, with future expansion into additional sectors and markets.

## Important

TenderAlert is an independent private service and does not represent or claim affiliation with PPDA or any government organization unless formally authorized.
